FAQ

Why are there ads?

Since version 1.1, Apila is free and supported by ads. We show them between levels or when you choose to watch one for coins, never during a game. There are no in-app purchases.

What does the "Allow tracking" prompt mean?

It is an Apple permission. If you allow it, ads may be more relevant to you; if you don't, you will still see ads, just less personalized. The game works the same either way. You can change it in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

I lost my progress. Can I get it back?

Progress is saved only on your device. If you delete the app or switch iPhones without a backup, it cannot be recovered from our servers, because we don't store it.

Do I need internet to play?

No. You can play offline; ads only load when you are connected.

Where can I see these animals?

When you complete each animal, the game shows "Where to see it": national parks and areas of Costa Rica where you are most likely to find it.

The game crashes or freezes. What should I do?

Close the app completely and open it again, make sure you have the latest version from the App Store, and restart your iPhone. If it keeps happening, write to us with your device details.
