Apila Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy explains what information is used when you play Apila, published by Víctor Rojas under the name biqmarketing ("we").

Information we collect

None. Apila does not ask you to create an account or give your name, email or phone number. Your progress (stars, coins, power-ups, streak, language and settings) is saved only on your device and is never sent to our servers.

Ads

Starting with version 1.1, Apila is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. Ads appear between levels, after you lose, or when you choose to watch one for a reward; never during a game. To show and measure ads, Google may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow it.
  • Technical data: device model, iOS version, language, IP address and the approximate location derived from it.
  • Ad interactions, for example whether an ad was shown or tapped, plus performance and crash data from the ad system.

This processing is governed by Google's privacy policy. Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Tracking and your choices

When you open the game, iOS may ask whether you allow tracking. If you say no, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized with your identifier. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Children

The first time you open Apila, we ask whether you are under 13. That answer is stored only on your device. If you say you are under 13, we do not ask for tracking permission and we tell Google to treat the session as child-directed: only non-personalized, family-safe ads are shown. You can change the answer in Privacy and support, inside the game.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, your iPhone schedules a local notification at 7 p.m. No information is sent to any server. You can turn it off in the game or in Settings.

How to delete your data

Delete the app from your iPhone and all game data is erased. For ad data, you can reset or limit the advertising identifier in iOS privacy settings.

Support emails

If you write to us, we use your email and what you tell us only to reply. We do not share or sell it. If a child writes to us, we use the message only to reply and will delete it at a parent's or guardian's request.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new date.
